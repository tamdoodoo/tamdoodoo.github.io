Livelyhood

About

Website design and build for Livelyhood, a Singapore live-commerce media network that makes original shopping shows with creators. The site speaks to three audiences at once — viewers, creators and brands — so each gets its own path, while one look ties them together: a deep red broadcast glow, a phone that stays in frame while the shows play, and motion that feels live, from a dot-matrix globe that spreads out from Singapore to flipbook icons and sections that come in as you scroll.
