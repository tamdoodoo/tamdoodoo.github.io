About Livelyhood

We make shopping
worth watching.

Livelyhood is a live commerce media network from Singapore. We create original live shopping shows, build them around creators people want to hear from, and give brands a real place inside them.

Why we started.

Shopping online got easier, but it also got quieter. Endless listings, star ratings and the same product photos everywhere.

Live brings back what a good shop assistant always gave you: someone who knows the product, shows it properly and answers your question before you've finished asking it. Livelyhood exists to turn that into a show worth tuning in for.
