For creators

Turn what you
love talking about
into a show.

A sponsored post ends when the
caption does. A show keeps going.

Livelyhood helps creators build a recurring live format around what they know and love, then brings the products, the brands & the production to make it work.

What you get

  • A format built with you.

    We develop the concept, the segments and the run of show together.

  • Products and brands, sorted.

    We source the products and handle the brand relationships, so you can focus on the show.

  • Production support.

    Set, lighting, camera and a producer with you on the day.

  • Paid for your time.

    Every show is paid, and how depends on the format.

What we ask

  • Show up on schedule.

    Live shows run at set times, so reliability matters.

  • Know the product.

    Prep before each show with the brief and samples we send.

  • Be honest on air.

    Only say what you actually believe about a product. Viewers can tell.

We look for people who are comfortable talking to camera and know their subject. A big following helps, but it isn't required.
