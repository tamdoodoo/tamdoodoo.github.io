NetZone Carbon Platform

About

Product UI design for NetZone, a carbon-management platform that helps businesses record, verify and report their greenhouse-gas emissions. I set up the design foundations and design system, then designed the screens: an intelligence dashboard, report management, an emissions data register, a step-by-step data entry flow and emission-reduction target setting — turning dense compliance data into clear, scannable interfaces.
