Threado

About

Website design and build for Threado, a Singapore custom apparel company with production in Vietnam. Threado makes uniforms, jerseys and merchandise for businesses, teams and communities, and the site has to make a technical process feel simple: five pages walk from what can be made, through how it gets made, to who it is for. The look is calm and tactile — warm paper, one clear blue, a serif with character — and the motion is quiet: thread lines behind the hero, icons that draw themselves, product panels that stack and a ring of photos that slows when you pick an audience.
