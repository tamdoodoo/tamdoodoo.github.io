TO.PEAK

About

UX/UI design for TO.PEAK, a Singapore brand that reimagined the toothpick as a focus tool for professionals. With no existing product category to lean on, I designed the e-commerce experience as one clear argument — the problem, the science, a comparison against coffee and energy drinks, then the lineup — so visitors reach the product pages already convinced. A strict black-and-white grid keeps it calm and credible, with blue reserved for actions.
